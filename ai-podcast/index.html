FAQ

What is an AI podcast?

An AI podcast can be a show about artificial intelligence or a show made using AI. Pale Blue Nexus is the first kind: Yohann Calpu interviews people working in AI and emerging technology.

Is Pale Blue Nexus made with AI?

It's not an AI-generated interview show. Yohann Calpu hosts the conversations with founders, investors and operators.

Which episode should I start with?

Start with Austin Armstrong for a nontechnical conversation about AI and work, Nikola Borisov for AI infrastructure, or Matt Blumberg for how AI changes enterprise content workflows.

How often do new episodes come out?

Check the episode archive for the latest interview, or follow the show on your preferred platform to see new episodes as they're published.

Where can I listen?

Listen on Apple Podcasts or Spotify, or watch the interviews on YouTube.
