About the Guest

Eliza Blank.

Thirteen years building The Sill, the houseplant company that put 'plant parent' into the cultural vocabulary. Twelve stores, $25M raised, exited in 2024. Now CEO of The Farmlink Project, which moved 150 million pounds of rescued produce in 2025.
